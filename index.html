Meet your host

Lance Johnson

Lance Johnson, P.E., brings a perspective shaped by engineering, real estate development, entrepreneurship, public service, and personal reinvention. His own path — including building an engineering company, navigating its collapse during the Great Recession, and rebuilding — informs the curiosity and humility he brings to every conversation.

Based in the Lawrence, Kansas area, Lance is also associated with LandInspired LLC. His conversations look beyond professional milestones to the people, moments, setbacks, and decisions that shape a life.
